Yardwork Services

Clean yard. Clean edges. Clean finish.

What We Handle

We focus on the most common home yardwork that makes a place look cared-for fast. If you’re unsure, text us photos and we’ll tell you what we can knock out.

Mowing & Trimming

  • Lawn mowing (front/back/side)
  • String trimming along fences & walls
  • Weed whacking around trees & beds
  • Quick tidy pass for overgrown corners
  • Cleanup blow-off (walks/drive/patio)

Edging & Curb Appeal

  • Sidewalk & driveway edging
  • Clean edges around rock/bed lines
  • Spray-out cracks (by request)
  • Debris sweep from curbs & gutters
  • “Before guests arrive” touch-ups

Weeds & Bed Cleanup

  • Hand-pull weeds in beds
  • Weed removal in rock areas
  • Dead plant pull-outs (small)
  • Bed shaping & light raking
  • Haul-off bagged debris (optional)

Shrub & Small Tree Care

  • Shrub trimming & shaping
  • Hedge trimming (light)
  • Small branch pruning (reach-safe)
  • Remove dead limbs (small)
  • Green waste bundling / bagging

Leaves & Seasonal Cleanup

  • Leaf blowing & raking
  • Bagging leaves & yard debris
  • Spring cleanups (winter leftovers)
  • Fall cleanups (leaf & bed reset)
  • Storm debris pickup (branches/limbs)

Haul-Off & Odd Jobs

  • Load + haul yard waste (by request)
  • Break down small piles of brush
  • Move light patio items for cleanup
  • Pet waste pickup (optional add-on)
  • Rental turnover yard reset
Heads up: We keep it safe and neighbor-friendly. We avoid high-risk tree work, climbing, or anything near power lines. If it needs a tree service, we’ll tell you up front.
