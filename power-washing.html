What We Clean

We offer exterior pressure washing to help your home look its best — with a focus on clean results, protecting surfaces, and being mindful about runoff.

Concrete & Flatwork

  • Driveways
  • Sidewalks
  • Patios
  • Front steps & entry pads
  • Light surface stain removal

Home Exteriors

  • Siding rinse (low pressure)
  • Brick & stone (surface rinse)
  • Garage doors
  • Porches & pillars
  • Fences (gentle wash)

Decks & Wood

  • Deck surface wash (gentle)
  • Stairs & railings rinse
  • Composite decking wash
  • Prep for staining (light wash)
  • Soft rinse to avoid damage

Extras & Add-ons

  • Trash bin rinse-out
  • Outdoor furniture wash
  • Playset wash
  • Light algae/mildew treatment
  • Before/after photo set
Runoff & surface safety: We use the right pressure for the surface and avoid harsh chemicals where possible. For jobs that require heavy chemical use or where storm-drain runoff is a concern, we’ll discuss options and do it the right way.
