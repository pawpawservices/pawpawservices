Mechanical Repair

Maintenance & repairs — honest, clean, and practical.

What We Handle

We focus on common maintenance and “keeps-you-on-the-road” repairs. If you’re unsure, text us what’s going on (photos help) and we’ll tell you if it’s in our lane.

Maintenance Basics

  • Oil & filter changes
  • Air / cabin filters
  • Battery testing & replacement
  • Wiper blades & bulbs
  • Fluid checks / top-offs

Brakes & Wheels

  • Brake pad replacement
  • Rotor replacement (when needed)
  • Brake noise inspections
  • Tire swaps (spare / flat assistance)
  • Visual suspension checks

Cooling & Overheating

  • Thermostat replacement
  • Radiator hoses & clamps
  • Coolant drain & refill
  • Cooling fan checks
  • Basic leak inspection

Starter, Alternator, Belts

  • Starter replacement
  • Alternator replacement
  • Serpentine belt replacement
  • Idler / tensioner checks
  • Charging system diagnostics

Sensors & Check Engine Light

  • OBD2 scan & code read
  • Basic troubleshooting guidance
  • Simple sensor replacements (as applicable)
  • Clear codes after repair
  • “Is it safe to drive?” assessment

Mobile Friendly Jobs

  • Parking lot / driveway service
  • Parts pickup (by request)
  • Pre-purchase basic inspections
  • Roadside “won’t start” checks
  • Fleet basics (filters, batteries, lights)
Heads up: To keep things safe and clean, we avoid jobs that require heavy lifts, major fluid spills, or specialty equipment. If it needs a full shop (transmission work, engine rebuilds, airbags, or major exhaust fabrication), we’ll tell you up front and can help point you in the right direction.
